# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Fans, festival/event organizers, journalists, and potential collaborators discovering YARA (Yaroslava Artiushkina) — a Ukrainian teenage vocalist from Odesa performing internationally (Tianjin Fest 2024 China, solo concert in France, national TV feature). Visitors arrive from social media or word of mouth wanting to quickly feel her stage presence and confirm she's a serious, internationally credentialed performer, not a hobbyist.

## Product Purpose

A single-page artist site that replaces a bare-bones placeholder. It exists to make a strong first impression using what the artist actually has — real stage photography, a promo video, and a growing catalogue of international achievements — rather than manufactured promo assets she doesn't have (no label, no merch line, no press photography budget). Success = a visitor leaves convinced she's the real deal within one scroll of the hero.

## Brand Personality

"Bright peacock with teeth" — vibrant, a little dramatic, proud of her Ukrainian identity, not shy about the spotlight, but still a teenager (not styled as an adult pop star). Three words: **vivid, theatrical, unguarded**.

## Anti-references

- **Not** the current madonna.com — that's a merch e-commerce grid for an album release, not a landing experience worth copying structurally.
- **Not** generic AI-landing-page grammar: no gradient text, no tiny tracked eyebrows above every section, no numbered 01/02/03 scaffolding, no cream/sand default background, no side-stripe card borders.
- **Not** overly mature/adult styling as the dominant visual note — she is a teenager; performance/stage imagery leads, high-fashion editorial shots are used sparingly and never as the hero/first-impression image.

## Design Principles

1. Real photography carries the site — three visual worlds (Stage / Studio / Life) are kept visually distinct rather than blended into one generic grid.
2. Motion is the substitute for a promo budget — GSAP/Lenis-driven scroll storytelling is the site's main "wow," not stock design flourishes.
3. Content she doesn't have yet (streaming music, expanded team page) ships as honest placeholders, not omitted or faked.
4. Identity-preservation: the existing YARA logo mark and orange signature color from her real press kit carry forward rather than being reinvented from scratch.
5. i18n is a first-class citizen (UA default / EN / CH), reflecting her actual international audience (Odesa, France, Tianjin).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for every GSAP/Lenis animation (crossfade/instant fallback).
- Body text contrast ≥4.5:1 against the dark base even with a vivid color system.
- All gallery images carry descriptive alt text (per-photo, not filename-derived).
